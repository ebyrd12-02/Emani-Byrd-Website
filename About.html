BUDT748-Industry Practicum
BUDT748 is an industry-sponsored capstone course where MSIS students synthesize their program knowledge to solve real-world business problems through information technology. Working directly with sponsoring firms, students build solutions that deliver measurable business value while sharpening their ability to navigate tech organizations, collaborate with key stakeholders, and present technical solutions to business executives.
